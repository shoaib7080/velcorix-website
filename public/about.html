About Velcorix - Engineering & Consultancy

Engineering-led consultancy and equipment rental solutions for critical industrial operations.

Leading Industrial Consultants in UAE

Velcorix is a UAE-based technical consultancy and equipment rental company supporting oil & gas, energy, and industrial projects. We work closely with operators, EPC contractors, and service providers across shutdowns, commissioning, and operational phases—delivering practical, field-driven solutions backed by reliable equipment and documentation across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the Northern Emirates.

Our team brings hands-on experience from live plant environments, ensuring that recommendations are executable, compliant, and aligned with project realities.
