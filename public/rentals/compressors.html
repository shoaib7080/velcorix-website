Air Compressors

Reliable air compression for industrial applications

Heavy-Duty Compression Solutions

Ensure continuous operation of your pneumatic tools and plant air systems with Velcorix's fleet of high-performance air compressors. We offer solutions tailored for both construction sites and industrial facilities.

Our inventory includes portable diesel compressors for mobility and efficient electric units for stationary applications. We also provide air dryers and high-pressure boosters for specialized testing requirements.

Key Offerings & Scope

Portable Diesel Compressors

185 CFM to 1600 CFM units for construction use

Electric Compressors

Stationary screw compressors for plant air supply

High-Pressure Units

Up to 25 bar for sandblasting and pressure testing

Air Treatment

Air dryers, filters, and moisture separators available

Typical Real-World Applications

Construction

Powering jackhammers and pneumatic drills.

Oil & Gas

Pipeline drying and pneumatic testing.

Sandblasting

High-flow air for surface preparation and painting.

Manufacturing

Reliable compressed air for factory automation.
