Flange Management & Support

Systematic Joint Integrity Assurance Solutions

Zero Leaks, Total Control

Effective flange management goes beyond just tightening bolts. It involves a systematic approach to identifying, tracking, and verifying every critical joint in your facility. Velcorix provides comprehensive management and technical support services to ensure absolute joint integrity.

From the initial tagging of flanges to the final handover of a leak-free system, our engineering teams manage the entire process, providing you with full traceability and peace of mind.

Scope / What’s Included

Database Management

Use of specialized software to log joint details and status.

Tagging System

Physical identification of joints to track progress (assembled, tightened, tested).

Work Pack Generation

Creation of detailed instruction sheets for field technicians.

QA/QC Handover

Compilation of final joint completion certificates for startup.

Typical Real-World Applications

Turnarounds

Managing thousands of joints during time-critical shutdowns.

Construction

Tracking joint progress during new plant erection.

Audits

Verifying compliance of existing systems to new standards.

Leak Investigations

Root cause analysis of failed joints.

Capability Details

Our technical support team acts as the brain behind the muscle of the bolting crews.

  • Software Integration: We utilize industry-leading flange management software to calculate torque values and track status.
  • Engineering Review: Review of client piping specifications to select correct gaskets, bolts, and lubricants.
  • On-Site Supervision: Deployment of subject matter experts to guide technicians and resolve queries instantly.

Compliance & Support

Procedures

Development of site-specific bolting procedures.

Progress Tracking

Daily reports on percentage completion and leak test readiness.

Training

On-site toolbox talks and best practice coaching.
