Refining and Distribution Operational Support

Refining & Distribution

Maintaining efficiency in downstream operations is essential for profitability. Velcorix offers specialized maintenance and support services for refineries, processing plants, and distribution networks.

We focus on minimizing downtime during turnarounds and ensuring that all process equipment operates at peak efficiency through rigorous maintenance and inspection programs.

Scope / What’s Included

Turnaround Support

Comprehensive planning and manpower for plant shutdowns.

Tank Services

Cleaning, inspection (API 653), and repair of storage tanks.

Catalyst Handling

Specialized loading and unloading of reactor catalysts.

Piping & Mechanical

Fabrication, modification, and repair of process piping.

Typical Real-World Applications

Refineries

Crude distillation unit maintenance.

Terminals

Product storage and loading arm maintenance.

Chemical Plants

Process vessel cleaning and inspection.

Pipelines

Pigging support for product transfer lines.

Capability Details

Our downstream services are executed with a focus on minimizing asset downtime and maximizing operational safety.

  • Turnaround Management: Proven methodologies for planning and executing complex plant shutdowns within tight windows.
  • Advanced NDT: Utilization of latest non-destructive testing technologies for corrosion mapping and flaw detection.
  • Safe Entry: Specialized confined space entry teams equipped with breathing apparatus and gas detection.

Compliance & Support

Process Safety

Strict adherence to PSM (Process Safety Management) guidelines.

API Standards

Services compliant with API 653, 510, and 570.

Environment

Waste management and spill containment protocols.

Qualified Crew

Multi-skilled technicians certified for hazardous areas.
