EPC Services

Engineering, Procurement, and Construction Excellence

Integrated EPC Solutions

Velcorix delivers high-impact Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) solutions designed to meet the rigorous demands of the Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, and Power sectors. We provide end-to-end project lifecycle management—from initial feasibility and Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) to final commissioning and handover.

Our integrated delivery model optimizes project schedules, minimizes risk, and ensures capital efficiency for complex brownfield and greenfield developments across the UAE and the region.

Key Offerings

Multi-Disciplinary Engineering

Comprehensive FEED and Detailed Engineering services enhancing constructability.

Global Procurement

Strategic sourcing, expediting, and material management with strict QA/QC.

Construction Execution

Safe and efficient civil, mechanical, and E&I construction services.

Commissioning Support

Structured pre-commissioning, functional testing, and startup assistance.

Applicable Projects

Upstream Oil & Gas

Wellhead tie-ins and gathering stations.

Refineries

Process unit upgrades and expansion projects.

Petrochemical

Plant modifications and reliability improvements.

Utilities

Water treatment and power distribution infrastructure.

Capability Details

Our execution strategy is built on a value assurance process that ensures control and visibility at every stage.

  • Engineering: Delivering precise Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) and detailed engineering packages focused on safety and constructability.
  • Procurement: Leveraging a global network of approved manufacturers to secure high-specification materials and equipment on schedule.
  • Construction: Implementing rigorous HSE protocols and quality control measures to deliver projects safely and to specification.
