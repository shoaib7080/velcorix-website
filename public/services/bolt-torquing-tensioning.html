Bolt Torquing & Tensioning

Integrated Solutions for Leak-Free Joint Integrity

Precision Bolting Solutions

The integrity of any piping system relies on the quality of its bolted connections. Velcorix provides expert hydraulic bolt torquing and tensioning services, ensuring every flange is secured to the precise specification required for safe, leak-free operation.

Torquing: We utilize calibrated hydraulic torque wrenches to apply controlled rotational force, ideal for standard flange connections.

Tensioning: For critical or large-diameter joints, we use hydraulic tensioners to apply direct axial load, eliminating friction and ensuring uniform gasket compression.

Why Choose Velcorix?

We combine advanced tooling with expert calculation software to eliminate guesswork and ensure joint integrity.

  • Certified Accuracy: All torque wrenches and pumps are calibrated to international standards (NIST traceable).
  • Engineering Support: We calculate precise torque values and pump pressures based on friction factors, bolt grade, and temperature.
  • Zero Leaks: Strict adherence to ASME PCC-1 guidelines and multi-pass tightening sequences (Star Pattern).
