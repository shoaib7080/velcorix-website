Crane Installation Services

Safe and Precise Erection of Lifting Equipment

Lifting Your Projects Higher

The installation of tower cranes and overhead (EOT) cranes requires a unique blend of structural engineering, mechanical precision, and rigorous safety discipline. Velcorix is a premier provider of crane erection, climbing, dismantling, and maintenance services.

Our certified teams ensure that your lifting assets are installed correctly, load-tested comprehensively, and certified for safe operation, whether on a high-rise construction site or a busy factory floor.

Scope / What’s Included

Erection & Dismantling

Complete assembly and disassembly of Tower and EOT cranes.

Climbing / Jumping

Safe height extension of tower cranes as buildings rise.

Commissioning

Load testing and functional checks to manufacturer specifications.

Maintenance

Preventive maintenance contracts and 24/7 breakdown support.

Typical Real-World Applications

Construction

Tower cranes for high-rise residential and commercial towers.

Industrial

Installation of EOT cranes in manufacturing plants and workshops.

Ports & Marine

Erection of gantry cranes and ship-to-shore loaders.

Logistics

Overhead systems for warehousing and material handling.

Capability Details

We bring together the right equipment and the right people for heavy lifting operations.

  • Certified Team: All riggers and supervisors are third-party certified for lifting operations.
  • Method Statements: Detailed lifting plans and risk assessments for every installation.
  • Tooling: Specialized hydraulic torque wrenches and alignment tools for precise assembly.

Compliance Standards

Load Testing

Proof load testing with certified water bags or weights.

DAC/Municipality

Approvals from local regulatory bodies.

Alignment

Laser alignment of crane rails for smooth operation.
