Pre-commissioning Manpower

Expert Teams for System Testing and Startup

Ensuring System Readiness

Before a plant goes live, it must be rigorously tested. Velcorix provides specialized manpower teams dedicated to pre-commissioning activities such as cleaning, gauging, drying, and pressure testing of pipelines and process systems.

Our personnel are familiar with the specific equipment and protocols required to verify system integrity before the introduction of hydrocarbons.

Scope / What’s Included

Process Engineers

For method statement development and system walk-downs.

Hydrotest Crews

Supervisors and technicians for pressure testing operations.

Pigging Specialists

Experts in launching and receiving cleaning pigs.

Leak Testing Teams

Nitrogen helium leak detection personnel.

Typical Real-World Applications

Pipeline Flooding

Controlled filling of pipelines for hydrotesting.

Chemical Cleaning

Staffing for chemical circulation skids.

Air Blowing

Operators for high-capacity air compressors.

Loop Checks

E&I technicians for instrument loop verification.

Capability Details

Pre-commissioning requires a specialized blend of operational knowledge and testing expertise.

  • Integrated Crews: Teams that can handle mechanical assembly and testing concurrently.
  • Documentation: Technicians trained in MC (Mechanical Completion) punch-listing.
  • Safety Protocols: High awareness of stored energy hazards during pressure tests.
