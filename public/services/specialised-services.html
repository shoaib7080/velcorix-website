Specialised Services

Tailored Solutions for Energy & Industry

Expertise Across Every Sector

Velcorix understands that the needs of an offshore platform differ vastly from those of a petrochemical refinery. Our specialized services are segmented to provide deep domain expertise relevant to each sector of the energy industry.

Whether exploring new fields upstream or refining products downstream, we deliver engineered solutions that enhance safety, efficiency, and productivity.
