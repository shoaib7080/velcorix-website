Oil & Gas Upstream/Downstream

Integrated Solutions for the Entire Energy Value Chain

Comprehensive Energy Support

Velcorix delivers end-to-end expertise across the Oil & Gas sector, continuously supporting operations from the wellhead to the refinery. By merging upstream field development with downstream processing support, we ensure seamless asset integrity and operational efficiency.

Upstream: We optimize exploration and production with rapid-response equipment rentals, wellhead maintenance, and skilled manpower for remote field operations.

Downstream: Our team ensures profitability in refining and gathering through specialized turnarounds, tank maintenance, and catalyst handling services.

Specialized Capabilities

Upstream Capabilities

Wellhead Maintenance

Routine inspection, greasing, and valve integrity checks to prevent leaks and downtime.

Drilling & Seismic Support

Logistics, campsite preparation, and auxiliary manpower for seismic surveys and drilling rigs.

Field Development

Early Production Facility (EPF) support and rapid deployment of power and compression rentals.

Downstream Capabilities

Refinery Turnarounds

Comprehensive planning, manpower, and mechanical services for plant shutdowns (TAR).

Tank & Vessel Services

API 653 tank inspections, cleaning, and repairs, including confined space entry.

Catalyst Handling

Expert loading and unloading of reactor catalysts under inert or atmospheric conditions.

Piping & Mechanical

Fabrication, spool replacement, and hot tapping support for process piping.

Applied Across the Value Chain

Onshore Rigs

Drilling rig and workover support.

Exploration

Remote survey logistics & camps.

Refineries

Process unit updates & maintenance.

Terminals

Storage & loading arm repairs.

Why Velcorix?

We bridge the gap between extraction and production, offering a unified standard of safety and quality wherever we operate.

  • Rapid Mobilization: Whether it's a remote desert wellhead or a coastal refinery, our teams deploy 24/7.
