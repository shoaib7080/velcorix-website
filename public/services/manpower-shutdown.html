Shutdown Manpower

Experienced Workforce for Turnaround Projects

Skilled Hands, Safe Execution

Plant shutdowns and turnarounds are high-pressure events where every hour counts. Velcorix alleviates the stress of staffing by providing highly skilled, vetted, and safety-conscious manpower.

From multi-disciplinary supervisors to hands-on technicians, our workforce is experienced in the unique challenges of shutdown environments, ensuring efficient execution and a safe return to operation.

Scope / What’s Included

Supervisors & Foremen

Experienced leaders offering mechanical, electrical, and civil oversight.

Mechanical Technicians

Fitters, fabricators, and millwrights for equipment overhaul.

Certified Welders

Code-qualified welders (ASME IX, AWS) for pipe and structural repairs.

Safety Personnel

HSE officers and fire watchers to maintain site safety.

Typical Real-World Applications

Scheduled Outages

Planned maintenance for refineries and power plants.

Emergency Shut

Rapid mobilization of repair crews for unplanned failures.

Valve Overhaul

Specialized teams for control valve and relief valve servicing.

Exchanger Retubing

Crews dedicated to heat exchanger bundle maintenance.
