Lifting Manpower

Certified Riggers and Operators for Heavy Lifts

Raising Standards in Lifting Operations

Safe lifting operations depend heavily on the competence of the personnel involved. Velcorix supplies third-party certified riggers, banksmen, and crane operators who are trained to handle complex lifts with precision and safety.

Whether it's a tandem lift or a confined space hoisting operation, our team ensures every movement is calculated and controlled.

Scope / What’s Included

Lifting Supervisors

Appointed Persons (AP) for planning and overseeing lifts.

Riggers (Level 1, 2, 3)

Competent crew for slinging and signaling.

Crane Operators

Licensed operators for mobile, crawler, and tower cranes.

Rigging Inspectors

Qualified to inspect lifting gear before use.

Typical Real-World Applications

Equipment Installation

Positioning of skids and heavy machinery.

Steel Erection

Structural steel lifting for construction.

Marine Lifting

Loading and unloading supply vessels.

Complex Lifts

Tandem lifts requiring coordinated crane operation.

Capability Details

We maintain a rigorous standard for all lifting personnel to mitigate the risks associated with suspended loads.

  • Certification: All personnel hold valid TPI or international agency cards.
  • Planning: Supervisors capable of creating detailed Lift Plans and Risk Assessments.
  • Communication: Standardized hand signals and radio protocols for clear direction.

Compliance Standards

LOLER

Compliance with Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations.

Competency

Verifiable training records for every crew member.

PPE

Standard Issue: High-vis, safety boots, and hard hats.
