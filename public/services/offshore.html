Offshore Services

Marine and Offshore Platform Support

Offshore Excellence

Operating in harsh marine environments requires reliable partners. Velcorix delivers specialized support for offshore platforms, FPSOs, and marine vessels, ensuring that equipment is always certified and operations are safe.

We understand the logistical challenges of offshore work. Our services are designed to maximize uptime and minimize the need for shore-based intervention.

Scope / What’s Included

Container Rentals

DNV 2.7-1 certified offshore containers and baskets.

Deck Machinery

Maintenance of winches, cranes, and anchor handling equipment.

Subsea Support

Provision of auxiliary equipment for diving and ROV operations.

Coating & Preservation

Marine-grade corrosion protection for topside assets.

Typical Real-World Applications

Vessel Support

Maintenance for OSVs and supply boats.

Platforms

Integrity management for fixed drilling and production rigs.

Subsea

Equipment support for underwater construction.

Workshops

Offshore containerized workshops for on-site repairs.

Capability Details

We ensure all equipment and personnel dispatched offshore meet the highest international standards of safety and reliability.

  • Certification: All lifting gear and containers come with valid third-party survey reports (TPI).
  • Training: Personnel hold valid BOSIET and HUET certifications for offshore travel.
  • Durability: Equipment is specially designed or treated to withstand harsh saline environments.

Compliance & Support

DNV Standards

Equipment built to DNV 2.7-1 / EN 12079.

Safety Training

Crew trained in sea survival and firefighting.

Traceability

Full documentation history for all rental assets.

Logistics

Seamless coordination with supply vessels and helicopters.
