Flange Management

Complete Joint Integrity Solutions

Ensuring Leak-Free Operations

Flange management is the cornerstone of mechanical joint integrity. At Velcorix, we offer a comprehensive suite of services designed to ensure that every bolted connection on your site is leak-free and compliant with international standards.

Our integrated approach combines field services like torquing and machining with robust engineering management, including tagging, tracking, and certification.

Our Flange Management Services

Explore our specialized range of bolting and machining solutions.

Bolt Torquing

Precision hydraulic tightening for critical mains and structural joints.

Bolt Tensioning

Accurate axial loading for high-pressure and large diameter bolts.

Flange Machining

On-site facing and restoration of damaged sealing surfaces.

Management & Support

Tagging, database management, and engineering technical support.

Typical Real-World Applications

Refinery Turnarounds

Large-scale unbolting and rebolting during shutdowns.

Pipeline Construction

Flange assembly for new oil and gas pipelines.

Offshore Platforms

Maintenance of critical high-pressure topside joints.

Power Stations

Steam turbine casing and valve maintenance.

Why Choose Velcorix?

We deliver assurance. Our flange management system is designed to give you complete visibility and confidence in your plant's integrity.

  • Certified Technicians: All personnel are trained to ECITB MJI10/19 standards.

  • Digital Reports: Instant generation of joint completion certificates for faster QA/QC sign-off.
