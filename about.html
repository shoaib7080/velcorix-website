About Us

Building Trust Through Quality and Innovation

Our Story

Velcorix was founded with a vision to revolutionize the mechanical trading and consultancy landscape in the UAE. Recognizing the need for a partner that offers both high-quality components and expert engineering advice, we established ourselves as a one-stop solution for industrial clients.

Over the years, we have built strong partnerships with global manufacturers, ensuring that our clients receive only the best products. Our consultancy wing is staffed by seasoned engineers who bring decades of experience to the table.

Our Mission

To provide superior mechanical solutions and consultancy services that empower our clients to achieve operational excellence and sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading mechanical partner in the Middle East, known for our integrity, innovation, and unwavering commitment to quality.
