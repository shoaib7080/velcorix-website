Upstream Support Solutions

Velcorix provides a suite of specialized services designed to optimize upstream operations. From exploration support to production enhancement, we help operators maximize recovery while ensuring the highest safety standards.

Our team understands the unique challenges of remote and demanding oilfield environments, delivering reliable equipment and skilled manpower exactly where it's needed.

Scope / What’s Included

Wellhead Maintenance

Routine inspection, greasing, and valve integrity checks.

Drilling Support

Provision of auxiliary equipment and manpower for rig operations.

Geological Survey Support

Field Development

Early production facility (EPF) support and equipment rental.
