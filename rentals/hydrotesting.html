Hydrotesting Equipment

Complete solutions for pressure testing and validation

Precision Pressure Testing

Velcorix offers a comprehensive range of certified hydrotesting equipment suitable for pipelines, pressure vessels, storage tanks, and valves. Our equipment is calibrated and maintained to ensure accurate test results for critical infrastructure.

From high-volume filling pumps to high-pressure testing units and precision recording instruments, we provide everything needed for a successful and compliant hydrotest operation.

Key Offerings & Scope

High Pressure Pumps

Diesel and air-driven units up to 30,000 PSI

Chart Recorders

Barton recorders for accurate pressure logging

Dead Weight Testers

Primary standard for pressure calibration and testing

Filling Pumps

High-volume centrifugal pumps for rapid filling

Typical Real-World Applications

Pipeline Testing

Pre-commissioning validation of oil & gas pipelines.

Storage Tanks

Integrity testing of new and repaired storage tanks.

Pressure Vessels

Code-compliant testing of boilers and reactors.

Valve Testing

Seat and shell testing of industrial valves.

Technical Specifications

Pressure Range Up to 30,000 PSI
Pump Type Triplex Plunger / Air Driven Liquid Pump
Flow Rate Variable (High Volume to Low Volume / High Pressure)
Recording Mechanical Chart / Digital Logging
Accuracy Up to 0.1% (with DWT/Digital Gauge)

Compliance & Support

Calibrated Equipment

All gauges and recorders come with current calibration certificates traceable to standards.

Complete Skid

Pumps are skid-mounted with relief valves, manifolds, and hoses ready for site.

Technician Support

Experienced hydrotest technicians available to operate equipment and certify charts.

Safety Accessories

Includes whip checks, pressure relief valves, and calibrated relief valves.
