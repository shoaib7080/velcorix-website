Testing Services

We provide comprehensive testing ensuring leak-free and pressure-resistant piping and vessel systems.

  • Hydro Testing: High-pressure water testing for strength and leaks.
  • Pneumatic Testing: Air/gas pressure testing for systems where water is not suitable.

Equipment Support

Supply and management of critical pre-commissioning equipment.

  • Blowers: High-capacity air movers for drying and purging.
  • Flanges & Fittings: Temporary and permanent sealing solutions for testing phases.

Crane Installation

Beyond rental, we offer expert installation services for overhead (EOT) and tower cranes. Our certified teams ensure precise assembly, alignment, and load testing to meet international safety standards.
